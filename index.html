How it works

Sereluniva is a web app, not an app-store download. It takes about a minute to set up.

  1. Buy onceYou receive a short guide with your app address.
  2. Add it to your home screenOpen the address in Safari (iPhone, iPad) or Chrome (Android, computer) and choose Add to Home Screen or Install.
  3. Use it anywhereIt opens like an app, full screen, and works offline.

Good to know

  • Your entries are stored on your own device. Save a backup file from Settings now and then.
  • The diary PIN is a screen lock, not encryption. Backup files are not encrypted.
  • Recipe import reads TikTok links, recipe websites, pasted text and screenshots. Instagram and Facebook posts cannot be imported.
  • Sereluniva cannot send notifications while it is closed. Use the calendar button on an event to get alerts from your phone's calendar app.
  • One purchase is for personal use by one person.
