Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Sereluniva is a planner app. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

Who we are

Sereluniva is made and sold by Simply Nurul, based in Singapore. You can reach us at simplynuru7@gmail.com.

What we collect

Sereluniva has no accounts and no tracking. We do not collect, receive or store your entries, photos, recipes or diary.

Where your data lives

Everything you write is stored only in your own browser on your own device. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app can delete it. Use Settings, then Save backup, to keep a copy. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere private.

The PIN

The diary PIN hides your diary on screen. It is a screen lock, not encryption. Keep your recovery code safe: we cannot recover your PIN.

Fonts and hosting

This website and the app may load fonts from Google Fonts when you are online, and our host (Vercel) may log standard technical data such as your IP address. We do not use this data.

Recipe links

If you paste a link in the app and tap Get recipe, the link is sent to our hosting service so it can fetch that page and return the recipe. Sereluniva does not store it, but the host may keep standard request logs. Pasted text and screenshots are read on your device.

Payments

Purchases are handled by our payment platform, which collects the details needed to process your order under its own privacy policy. We receive your name and email for order support.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Sereluniva works with your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: simplynuru7@gmail.com
